Textile Selection Criteria
  • Structural Analysis: Matching fabric weight (GSM) with garment silhouette (flared vs structured).
  • Bespoke Sourcing: Sourcing direct-from-weaver silk from Kanchipuram and South Indian handloom clusters.
  • Lining & Reinforcement: Selection of canvas, horsehair braid, and breathable linings for longevity.
  • Quality Auditing: Shrinkage testing, color fastness assessment, and fiber burn tests.
